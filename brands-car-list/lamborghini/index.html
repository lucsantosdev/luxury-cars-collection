Born in Sant'Agata Bolognese

Since 1963, Automobili Lamborghini has been creating dreams. Founded by Ferruccio Lamborghini with the vision to build the perfect grand touring car, we've become synonymous with extreme performance and Italian passion.

Every Lamborghini is handcrafted in Sant'Agata Bolognese, Italy, where tradition meets innovation. Our commitment to pushing boundaries has created some of the most iconic supercars in automotive history.

"I want to build a perfect car - a grand touring car that's faster, more beautiful, and better than anything Ferrari has ever made."
— Ferruccio Lamborghini

Innovation

Technology Born from Passion

Every Lamborghini incorporates cutting-edge technology developed through decades of motorsport experience and relentless pursuit of performance perfection.

V12 POWER

The legendary naturally aspirated V12 engine delivers an unmistakable symphony and instant throttle response that defines the Lamborghini experience.

HYBRID TECHNOLOGY

Revolutionary plug-in hybrid systems combine V12 power with electric motors for unprecedented performance and efficiency.

AERODYNAMICS

Active aerodynamic systems adapt in real-time, optimizing downforce and drag for maximum performance in every driving condition.

CARBON FIBER

Extensive use of carbon fiber monocoque construction delivers exceptional rigidity while minimizing weight for optimal power-to-weight ratio.

4-WHEEL DRIVE

Advanced all-wheel drive systems with active torque vectoring deliver incredible traction and handling dynamics.

LDVI SYSTEM

Lamborghini Dinamica Veicolo Integrata controls every vehicle system in real-time for predictive and intuitive driving dynamics.
