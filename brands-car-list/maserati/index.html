Over a Century of Italian Passion

Since 1914, Maserati has been synonymous with Italian luxury, innovation, and performance. Founded in Bologna by the Maserati brothers, our legacy is built on a passion for racing and a commitment to excellence.

From the legendary 250F that dominated Formula 1 to today's MC20 supercar, Maserati continues to blend motorsport heritage with contemporary design and cutting-edge technology.

Handcrafted in Modena

Every Maserati is a masterpiece of Italian craftsmanship, meticulously assembled by hand in our historic plant in Modena. Our artisans combine centuries-old traditions with modern innovation to create automotive works of art.

From hand-stitched Poltrona Frau leather interiors to precision-engineered performance components, every detail is crafted with care and passion.
